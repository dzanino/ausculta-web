1. What the app keeps on the device

Ausculta collects no personal data. On the device it stores only these settings, which it sends nowhere:

  • Play counter: which atlas examples you have already played, for the “Heard X of Y” overview and the “Not yet heard” filter. It concerns only the atlas examples, not you.
  • Acceptance of the introductory notice: that you have read that Ausculta is not a medical device.
  • Purchase status (whether you have Ausculta Pro): so that Pro also works offline.

These settings are part of your device backup if you have it turned on. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. What the app does not do

  • It does not use the microphone and picks up or records no sound.
  • It has no account or server and sends nothing.
  • It collects no analytics, device identifiers or location.
  • It contains no ads or third-party SDKs.
  • The atlas and the ECG examples are part of the app; nothing is downloaded from the internet.
  • Links to other apps by the author only open the App Store; the app sends nothing.

3. In-app purchases

You buy Ausculta Pro and voluntary contributions through the App Store. The payment and your Apple Account are handled solely by Apple under its own privacy policy. The app receives no payment details, name, email or other personal data: the App Store only tells it whether you have Ausculta Pro, and the app stores this on the device. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Apple.

4. Permissions

Ausculta asks for no permissions: it needs no microphone, camera, location, photos, contacts or access to the Health app.

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and collects no personal data about anyone.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Because the app collects no personal data and sends nothing, the controller has no access to any of your data and does not process it. You delete the settings stored on the device by uninstalling the app. Questions: admin@breathune.eu.

7. Changes

We will publish changes to this policy on this page with the date of the update.
