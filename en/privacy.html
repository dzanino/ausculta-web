1. What the app keeps on the device

Ausculta collects no personal data. On the device it stores only these settings, which it sends nowhere:

  • Play counter: which atlas examples you have already played, for the “Heard X of Y” overview and the “Not yet heard” filter. It concerns only the atlas examples, not you.
  • Daily practice progress (from version 2.2): which atlas examples you named correctly or wrongly in practice, when they are due for review and the recent answers for the accuracy overview by type of sound. It describes only your answers about the atlas recordings, nothing about your body.
  • Acceptance of the introductory notice: that you have read that Ausculta is not a medical device.
  • Purchase status (whether you have Ausculta Pro): so that Pro also works offline.
  • Recordings from the Listen tab (Android only): the WAV sound files you saved yourself by tapping Record, in the app's private folder on the phone. Other apps have no access to them.

These settings are part of your device backup if you have it turned on (iCloud or Google). Recordings on Android are excluded from backups and from the transfer to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes the settings and the recordings.

2. Microphone and recordings (Android only)

Ausculta for iPhone and iPad does not use the microphone. Ausculta for Android has a Listen tab for listening practice: it plays the sound of the phone's microphone, or of a connected wired or USB microphone, live to your headphones.

  • When: the microphone runs only in the Listen tab, while you listen. The app asks for access only when you first tap Start listening, and explains beforehand what it needs it for. It does not use the microphone in the background or in other tabs.
  • What happens to the sound: on the phone the sound is only filtered (Bell, Diaphragm, Wide), amplified and sent to your headphones, and a waveform is drawn from it on the screen. The app measures, counts or recognises nothing in it and sends it nowhere.
  • Recordings: sound is saved only when you tap Record. A recording is a WAV file in the app's private folder on the phone; other apps have no access to it. Recordings are not part of the backup to your Google Account or of the transfer to a new phone.
  • Sharing: a recording leaves the phone only when you share it yourself (Share, then the app or person you choose). From then on, the app or service you choose is responsible for it.
  • Deleting: delete a recording in the app (Listen › Recordings › Delete); uninstalling the app deletes all of them.
  • Withdrawing access: you can remove microphone access at any time in the Android settings (Settings › Apps › Ausculta › Permissions › Microphone). The atlas, the quiz and the other features work without it.
  • Other people: if you listen to or record someone else, for example a fellow student, do so only with their consent. The recording stays with you; the controller has no access to it.

3. What the app does not do

  • On iPhone and iPad it does not use the microphone and picks up or records no sound. On Android it does not use the microphone outside the Listen tab or in the background, and measures or evaluates nothing in the sound.
  • It has no account or server and sends nothing.
  • It collects no analytics, device identifiers or location.
  • It contains no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. On Android it uses only the Google Play libraries for purchases and for rating the app in the store.
  • The atlas and the ECG examples are part of the app; nothing is downloaded from the internet.
  • Links to other apps by the author only open the App Store or Google Play; the app sends nothing.

4. In-app purchases

You buy Ausculta Pro and voluntary contributions through the App Store on iPhone and iPad and through Google Play on Android. The payment and your Apple Account or Google Account are handled solely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The app receives no payment details, name, email or other personal data: the store only tells it whether you have Ausculta Pro, and the app stores this on the device. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Apple or Google.

5. Permissions

Ausculta for iPhone and iPad asks for no permissions: it needs no microphone, camera, location, photos, contacts or access to the Health app. Ausculta for Android asks only for the microphone, and only when you first tap Start listening in the Listen tab; it needs no camera, location, photos, contacts or Health Connect. If you do not allow microphone access, or remove it later, only the Listen tab stops working.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and collects no personal data about anyone.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Because the app collects no personal data and sends nothing, the controller has no access to any of your data or recordings and does not process them. You delete the settings stored on the device by uninstalling the app; on Android you can also delete recordings directly in the app. Questions: admin@breathune.eu.

8. Changes
