1. What data the app handles

  • Microphone audio (iPhone or AirPods) – processed in real time on the device for filtering, amplification and display of the waveform and spectrum. It is not stored until you press Record.
  • Recordings (WAV) and metadata (time, listening site, notes) – only in the app folder on the device; they are backed up with the iPhone according to your iCloud backup settings.
  • Settings (probe mode, filter, gain, consent to the notice) – local on the device.

2. What the app does not do

  • It sends no audio or recordings to any server.
  • It collects no analytics, device identifiers or location.
  • It contains no ads or third-party SDKs and requires no account.

3. Sharing

If you use the Share button, the iOS system dialog hands the chosen file to the app you select (Mail, Messages, AirDrop…). From then on that service's rules apply. Recordings of body sounds can be sensitive personal data, so share them with care. Do not share recordings of other people without their consent.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Because data never leaves your device, the controller has no access to it and does not process it. Erasure: delete the recordings in the app or uninstall the app. Questions: ausculta@breathune.eu.
